Wujia Kitchen

Wujia Kitchen is a small home kitchen in Sugar Land, Texas. This site describes the internal order tool we use for our own business.

The tool reads our own group-order result emails (read-only) from our own Gmail inbox, so we can prepare orders and delivery lists. It is used only by our own staff. It is not offered to the public.
